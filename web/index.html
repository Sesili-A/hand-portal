Hand Portal

Filters, drawing and stickers, all controlled with your hands at the same time. Nothing leaves your computer: the camera is processed in this browser tab.

  • Open a portalMake an L with thumb and index on both hands. The filter shows inside the frame, including face filters.
  • Change filterHold a pinch on empty space, or make a fist.
  • DrawPoint with your index finger, thumb tucked in. Hover the colour buttons at the top to change colour.
  • Move stickersPinch a sticker to drag it. Pinch it with both hands to resize and rotate.

Keys: N / B filter · C clear drawing · P snap photo · R reset stickers · H hand lines · S screenshot
