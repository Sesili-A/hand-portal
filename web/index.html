Live AI

Inside the portal, the camera is replaced by a real-time AI restyle from Decart. This sends your camera video to Decart while Live AI is on.

If you still look too much like your real self and not enough like a drawn character, switch to lucy-2.6: it changes the picture more.

Get a key at platform.decart.ai (new accounts get free credits). Billing is per second while Live AI is connected; it switches itself off after 60 s without a portal.

Hand Portal

Filters, drawing and stickers, all controlled with your hands at the same time. Nothing leaves your computer: the camera is processed in this browser tab.

  • Open a portalMake an L with thumb and index on both hands. The filter shows inside the frame. Two people can each open their own.
  • AnimeThe free Anime filter (on by default) turns the inside of the frame into a cartoon with big anime eyes. For a full AI anime redraw, turn on Live AI at the bottom (needs a Decart API key).
  • Change filterHold a pinch on empty space, or make a fist.
  • DrawPoint with your index finger, thumb tucked in. Hover the colour buttons at the top to change colour.
  • Move stickersPinch a sticker to drag it. Pinch it with both hands to resize and rotate.

Keys: A Live AI · N / B filter · C clear drawing · E eraser · +/− pen size · P snap photo · R reset stickers · H hand lines · F fps · S screenshot
